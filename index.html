Private Equity

Holdings, valuations and exits.

  • Control and minority positions
  • Cap tables with import checks
  • Valuation history
  • IRR, TVPI, DPI and RVPI

Venture Capital

Rounds and returns.

  • Seed to growth rounds and follow-ons
  • SAFEs and convertible notes
  • Ownership and pro rata tracking
  • Founder reporting each quarter

Multi Strategy

One record across both.

  • Any mix of instruments in a fund
  • One company record throughout
  • Reporting across vehicles
  • Stages, criteria and roles per fund

Family Offices

  • Direct deals and fund commitments together
  • Reporting for the family and its advisers
  • Access for each member and adviser
  • Documents and approvals in one place
